Q: But not all businesses are capable or willing to adopt this change of ways in their businesses. How do you think Street food vendors will adopt this?
A: Given that the devices we have been using continuously change, and not all can afford this change. As a developer, this system will and should be capable of handling older systems.

Q: What happens to our data if Cloud tends to fail, will it be safe or will it break?
A: The system can be ran offline. Everything that happened offline or a sudden outage will be automatically cached.

Data that were on the cloud will be saved locally on your end, making sure that everything feels safe.
